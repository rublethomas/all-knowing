## Update: no Motorola, thickness near S25 Edge (~5.8mm)

Motorola Edge 70 is out. Only a few Android phones are this thin:
- Galaxy S25 Edge (5.8mm): the only one with a flagship-level camera (200MP main). Refurb/open-box ~$460-620 US. Weak battery (~3,900mAh), no telephoto.
- Tecno Pova Slim (5.95mm, 156g, India): budget, cameras described as "satisfactory", heats up under load. Cheap.
- Infinix Hot 60 Pro+ (~5.95mm, 5,160mAh, India): budget, laggy, camera not a strength.
- Tecno Spark Slim (5.93mm): budget.
- iPhone Air (5.6mm) is iOS, so excluded.
- Foldables (Z Fold7 unfolded) are thinner but cost far more.

Conclusion: thin + great camera = Galaxy S25 Edge, almost alone. Budget slim phones have mediocre cameras.
