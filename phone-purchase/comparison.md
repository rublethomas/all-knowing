# Comparison: Galaxy S25 Edge vs Realme (as of 2026-11-05)

Assumed "Realme" = Realme GT 8 Pro and Realme 16 Pro (the two found in India). Prices unverified; Rs converted at ~Rs 95/USD.

| | Galaxy S25 Edge | Realme GT 8 Pro | Realme 16 Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thickness / weight | 5.8mm / ~163g | ~8.2mm / 214-218g | 7.8mm / 192g |
| Chip (performance) | Snapdragon 8 Elite, 12GB RAM, AnTuTu ~2.2M | Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, up to 16GB | Dimensity 7300 Max (mid-range) |
| Main camera | 200MP, praised | Ricoh GR-tuned system; strong, praised | 200MP Samsung HP5 main, 8MP ultrawide |
| Zoom | No telephoto (2x crop from 200MP) | 200MP 3x telephoto | No telephoto |
| Battery / charging | 3,900mAh, 25W (weak; tests range 6h screen-on to ~16h PCMark) | 7,000mAh, 120W wired, 50W wireless (reviews: ~a day to 24h browsing) | 7,000mAh, 80W |
| Price | $502 refurb (Back Market Premium) / $749 new black | Rs 72,999 (~$765) at launch | ~Rs 44,999-46,999 (~$475-495) |

Notes
- GT 8 Pro: best camera and performance among the Realmes, but ~2.4mm thicker and ~55g heavier than the Edge, and costs more than the Edge.
- 16 Pro: cheap with big battery, but mid-range chip (reviewers: underwhelming, runs warm); fails the multitasking requirement. Digit: "good phone let down by its price".
- Ultra Mobile / T-Mobile band support for Indian Realme models not verified.
